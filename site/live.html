One diamond.
One V. One W.

The wonmor mark starts as a single cut diamond. It splits into a crown and a V. Then the V doubles: the two halves slide apart until their inner arms lock, the crowns join into one, and the V becomes a W.

01Construction

  1. One diamondOne grid, 100 × 100.
  2. Split the girdleA crown on top, a V below.
  3. Cut the VThe notch drops to 62 % of the height.
  4. V becomes WThe V doubles at offset 64: inner arms coincide, the crowns join.

04Wordmark

  1. 10One stroke weight. Every letter uses the width of the mark’s arm.
  2. 5Letter spacing is half the stroke, the same gap that splits the crown from the V.
  3. xThe crown sits on the x-height, the tip of the W drops below the baseline.
  4. wThe mark is the w. The lockup reads mark + onmor; the word never repeats the w.

The wordmark is drawn, not typed. Interface text uses Plus Jakarta Sans (SIL OFL 1.1), self-hosted.
