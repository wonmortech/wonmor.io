FAQ

Questions sales leaders ask first.

Does wonmor replace our call recorder?

No. wonmor reads recordings and transcripts from the tool you already use and adds the win-loss layer on top.

How long does setup take?

Connecting the CRM and call recorder takes about an hour. The first report covers your last two quarters of closed deals.

Who can see call content?

Access follows roles you set. Reps see their own deals; leaders see the team; quotes can be limited to managers.

Can we keep our own loss categories?

Yes. Enterprise lets you map reasons to your own taxonomy so reports match the board deck.
