Two diamonds.
Two V. One W.

The wonmor mark starts as a pair of cut diamonds. Each diamond splits into a crown and a V. Slide them together until the inner arms meet, and the crowns become one and the two V become a W.

01Construction

  1. Two diamondsOne grid, 100 × 100 each.
  2. Split the girdleA crown on top, a V below.
  3. Cut the VThe notch drops to 62 % of the height.
  4. MergeOffset 64: inner arms coincide, the crowns join, W appears.
