Products · Experiments

med

An experiment in medical learning: structured study material and practice by specialty.

Experiment Visit med.dtwin.uz(opens in a new tab)

Overview

med is an experimental study platform for medicine. It covers 11 topics across 5 specialties with ICD-10 codes, short clinical guidelines, a drug reference without dosages, a practice exam and a personal progress page. All content is demo material and is labelled as such.

Release level
WRL-1 · Prototype
Live since
Oct 30, 2026
Website
med.dtwin.uz

What it does

  1. 01

    Structured topics

    Each topic carries its ICD-10 code, a short guideline and links to related topics.

  2. 02

    A careful drug reference

    The pharmacology section describes drugs without dosages, so it cannot be read as a prescription.

  3. 03

    Practice and progress

    A short exam checks understanding, and a progress page shows what has been studied.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose a topic

    Search or browse by specialty to open a topic.

  2. 2

    Read the guideline

    Each page gives the code, the key points and related drugs.

  3. 3

    Test yourself

    The practice exam records results on your progress page.
