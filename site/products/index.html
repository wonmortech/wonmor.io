Core

  • dtwin Beta A personal life planner built around the five daily prayers, with an MCP server of 96 tools so Claude and other assistants can work with your day. dtwin.uz

Agents and infrastructure

  • Gateway MVP One control plane for every AI agent’s tools: personal tokens, masking of personal data, a full audit log and a kill switch. gateway.dtwin.uz
  • Tunnel MVP Self-hosted tunnels that put a local service on a public address, with a personal token for every user. tunnel.dtwin.uz
  • Fam Preview Self-hosted bug reports with session replay, a browser extension and an MCP server, so your agents can read the bug instead of guessing. fam.dtwin.uz

Language

  • bitg Open source · v0.3.1 Type Uzbek in the 2026 Latin alphabet in any app, on Windows, Linux and macOS, without breaking English. One Rust core, open source. bitg.uz

Telegram

  • TeleTwin MVP A company layer for Telegram: shared work accounts, roles, and one click to revoke an employee’s access. tele.dtwin.uz

Experiments

  • shop Experiment An experiment in small-business commerce: a storefront and order flow built on the same stack as the rest of dtwin. shop.dtwin.uz
  • gym Experiment An open-source workout diary: a library of exercises, ready-made plans and your progress, with no account needed. gym.dtwin.uz
  • med Experiment An experiment in medical learning: structured study material and practice by specialty. med.dtwin.uz

Built with others

  • Oilaviy In production Restaurant ordering on the web and inside Telegram, with live order status for guests and staff. oilaviyshaharcha.uz
  • Primus Library In production A 3D asset library and production board for an animation studio: models, versions, episodes and offsite backups. primus-library.dtwin.uz
  • Halvet Concept A premium site concept for hammam and sauna builders, with a WebGL dome and fog. Concept build with stock imagery. halvet.dtwin.uz
  • operobot Prototype An outbound call assistant designed around local rules for consent and calling hours. operobot2.dtwin.uz

3D and spatial

  • faktura-twin In production A live 3D digital twin of a working office: servers, services and team activity in one scene that agents can also query. faktura.dtwin.uz
  • Attendo MVP Face-recognition attendance on Hikvision terminals, rewritten for clarity: who came, when, and what the payroll needs to know.
  • xnosir.io Live The founder’s portfolio, built as a WebGL descent through the systems behind the founder’s work, in three languages. xnosir.io
