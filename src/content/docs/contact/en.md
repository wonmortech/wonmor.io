## What we do not offer

There is no email address and no phone number for Wonmor, and no contact form on this site. Wonmor is run by one founder, and keeping to one chat channel and public issues is how messages get answered instead of lost.

If your question is about a specific product, such as dtwin or bitg, name it in the first message so it reaches the right place quickly.
