## How we will run them

- **Free to attend.** None of the planned events is meant to be ticketed.
- **Announced with real details.** An event is listed with a date only once the date, place and format are confirmed.
- **Uzbek first.** Sessions are planned in Uzbek, with Russian and English where the audience needs it.
- **Material published afterwards.** Slides, links and recordings go up on this page when the speakers agree.
