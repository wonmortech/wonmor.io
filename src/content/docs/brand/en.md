## The mark

The Wonmor mark tells one short story. It starts as a single diamond. The diamond is cut into a V. Then the V splits in two, and the two V's standing side by side become a W.

The finished mark is those two V's. Each V is built from the same two parts: a flat top bar (the crown) and a chevron below it. The left V sits slightly behind, at 65% opacity; the right V is solid. Keeping the left one lighter is what lets the mark read both as two V's and as one W.

Because the mark *is* the letter w, the full lockup is the mark followed by **onmor**. Never set the word "wonmor" next to the mark: that spells the W twice.

## Lockups

The wordmark "onmor" is not typed in a font. It is drawn from the same geometry as the mark: the top of the mark lines up with the top of the lowercase letters, and the point of the V sits on their baseline. There are two lockups:

| Lockup | Mark | Wordmark | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|
| On light | Wonmor Blue | Ink | Paper, white and light photographs |
| On dark | Wonmor Blue | White | Ink, black and dark photographs |

Use the mark alone where space is tight, such as app icons, avatars and favicons. Use the lockup where the name needs to be read.

## Clear space and minimum size

The figures in this section are guidance, not hard rules; use judgement for unusual formats.

- **Clear space.** Keep an empty margin around the mark or lockup at least as tall as the crown, the top bar of one V. That is roughly a quarter of the mark's height. Nothing else, including text, edges and other logos, should enter that margin.
- **Minimum size, screen.** Mark: 16 px tall, which is the size of the favicon. Lockup: 120 px wide, so the letters of "onmor" stay clearly separate.
- **Minimum size, print.** Mark: 5 mm tall. Lockup: 25 mm wide.
- **Icons.** For app icons and avatars use the supplied rounded-square icons; they already include the right padding.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the supplied files as they are | Redraw the mark or retype the wordmark in any font |
| Put the blue mark on light or dark backgrounds with clear contrast | Recolour the two V's in different colours, or use gradients |
| Use the white mark on Wonmor Blue or on dark photographs | Add shadows, glows, outlines, bevels or 3D effects to the static mark |
| Keep the left V lighter than the right | Merge the two V's into one solid shape, or swap their order |
| Scale the mark proportionally | Stretch, squeeze, rotate or crop it |
| Leave clear space around it | Place it on busy patterns where it stops being legible |
| Write "Wonmor" next to the mark only when the lockup is not used | Combine the mark with "wonmor" to make "wwonmor" |

## Naming

- **Wonmor** is written with a capital W in running text. It is a brand, so do not add "Inc.", "LLC" or similar.
- **dtwin** is always lowercase, even at the start of a sentence or in a headline.
- Product names keep their own spelling: **bitg** and **operobot** in lowercase, **TeleTwin** with two capitals.
- Say "dtwin, built and supported by Wonmor" when you need to describe the relationship. dtwin is a product ecosystem; Wonmor is the brand behind it.

## The brand film

The brand film is a 19.4-second animation of the mark's story: the diamond, the cut, the split into two V's and the letters of the lockup arriving. It is rendered from code, from the same geometry files as the logo. There are two versions: 16:9 at 1920×1080 for screens and presentations, and a vertical 9:16 version for phones and stories.

You may show the film in coverage of Wonmor or its products, as long as you do not edit, recolour or re-time it, or add other branding to it.
