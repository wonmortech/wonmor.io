Wonmor's work is the work of its founder, Nosirbek Xakimov. On November 9, 2026, that work got a site of its own. It first went live at xnosir.dtwin.uz and moved to its own domain, xnosir.io, the same day; the old address now redirects there.

## System Descent

The site is built as a descent through the layers of a software system. It opens with a boot screen that reads a few harmless facts from your own browser — the browser, the graphics card and your time zone — and then grants access. From there you scroll down through the stack:

- the surface, with the name;
- the application layer, drawn as a torus knot;
- a strand of DNA, then a city of server racks;
- a spinning disk;
- the projects, with a career history laid out as `git log --career` on a globe;
- and finally the root.

A small terminal is available throughout. The site reads in Uzbek, Russian and English; the terminal answers in English.

## Built to be found

The launch included the parts that are easy to skip: Open Graph and Twitter cards, structured data, a sitemap, `robots.txt`, `security.txt`, a `noscript` fallback and a real 404 page.

A later release added a small content platform. Markdown files become static pages, with search, an RSS feed and a sitemap generated at build time. Some sections — such as trips, photos and books — are marked "coming soon" and stay empty until there is real material to put in them. Nothing on the site is filled in for the sake of looking complete.
