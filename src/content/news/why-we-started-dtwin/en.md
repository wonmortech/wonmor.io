dtwin did not start as a product. On October 23, 2026, the founder sat down to rebuild, for personal use, a planner organised around prayer — a "notebook for the mind" holding habits, thoughts, posts and a daily journal in one place, with friends to be invited later.

## The idea behind it

Most planners divide the day at midnight. For someone whose day is shaped by the five prayers, that line is in the wrong place. So we made one decision first: in dtwin, a day runs from Maghrib to Maghrib, the way it does in the Islamic calendar, and prayer times are the anchors that everything else is placed around.

A second decision followed from the first. Worship is personal. dtwin has no prayer leaderboards, no public streaks and no feed of who prayed what. What you record about your worship is for you.

## The first days

The first version was a .NET 10 API with a React 19 web app: a Today page, habits, notes and settings, with sign-in through Telegram. The same evening the project was renamed from its working title to dtwin, and the domain dtwin.uz and a server in Uzbekistan were bought.

The next day brought:

- a year-in-review page that compares a period with the same dates a year earlier;
- four interface languages — Uzbek in Latin and Cyrillic, Russian and English — with the Cyrillic version transliterated automatically;
- friends and invitations, and prayer reminders a set number of minutes before each prayer, sent through the Telegram bot.

Three days after the first line of code, dtwin.uz opened to the public.
