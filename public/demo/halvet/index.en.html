      1. OverviewPool water travels this route several times a day: out of the basin, through the equipment and back again.
      2. SkimmerCollects leaves, dust and oils from the surface — this is where the suction line begins.Ø 50–63 mm PVC
      3. Main drainDraws water from the bottom and keeps the whole basin circulating, leaving no dead zones.Anti-entrapment grate, calculated velocity
      4. Pump and pre-filterThe basket catches coarse debris; the pump drives water through the whole system.Variable-speed drive — quiet and efficient
      5. Sand filterWater passes down through the sand bed and fine particles stay behind. When pressure rises, it is backwashed.Six-way multiport valve and pressure gauge
      6. Heat exchangerTransfers the boiler’s heat to the pool water without the two circuits mixing. Water stays at 26–28 °C all year.Stainless steel, plate or shell-and-tube
      7. DosingSensors measure pH and chlorine; pumps add micro-doses until both are in range.pH 7.2–7.6 · chlorine 1–3 mg/l
      8. Return inletsClean, warm water flows evenly back into the basin; inlet placement eliminates “dead” zones.
      9. Underwater lightingLED fixtures run on safe extra-low voltage — at night the pool glows from within.12 V, IP68

      Questions

      Frequently asked

      Can we commission the engineering alone?

      Yes. We design and install the pump and filtration, heating, electrical or ventilation on their own, even when another team handles construction.

      Do you renovate existing pools and hammams?

      Yes. We first visit to assess the equipment, pipework and waterproofing, then set out in writing what needs replacing.

      How long does a project take?

      It depends on scope and materials. The estimate lists the duration of each stage separately.

      Who looks after the pool water?

      Auto-dosing holds pH and chlorine on its own. We continue to handle filter backwashing, chemistry and seasonal service after handover.

      How much space does a hammam need?

      A small private hammam fits in a single room of the house. On a site visit we review the foundations, water supply, sewer and electrical service, then give you a precise solution.

      Can I choose the materials myself?

      Of course. We select marble, mosaic and timber samples together.
